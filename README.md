# CNN Concept Explanation

## What is Convolution?

Convolution is a mathematical operation where small filters scan across an image to detect important visual patterns such as edges, textures, and shapes.

## Why is Pooling Used?

Pooling reduces image dimensions while keeping important features. This helps reduce computation and prevents overfitting.

## Why is ReLU Commonly Used?

ReLU introduces non-linearity into the network and helps models train faster by avoiding vanishing gradient problems.

## Why are CNNs Better than Feed-Forward Networks for Images?

CNNs preserve spatial relationships between pixels and automatically learn visual features. Feed-forward networks flatten images and lose spatial information.
